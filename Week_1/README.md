# Week 1 – Airline Flight Delay Dashboard

## Objective
Initial analysis and preparation of the 2024 airline flight delay dataset for the dashboard.

## Team Contributions

- Member 1 – Data Cleaning
- Member 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Member 3 – Data Visualization
- Member 4 – Dashboard Planning

## Week 1 Work


### Member 1 – Data Cleaning
- Cleaned and preprocessed the flight dataset.
- Checked missing values, duplicates, data types and outliers.

### Member 2 – Exploratory Data Analysis
- Examined the structure and characteristics of the dataset.
- Analyzed airline distribution.
- Studied cancelled flights.
- Analyzed departure delays.
- Compared delay rates across airlines.
- Examined monthly flight distribution.

### Member 3 – Data Visualization
- Initial visualization planning for flight delays and related factors.

### Member 4 – Dashboard Planning
- Identified dashboard requirements, KPIs and possible layout.
